      We roast on Tuesdays and Fridays.

      An angithi is the small coal hearth that kept kettles hot in every Indian home. We named the roastery after it because good coffee starts with fire and patience.

      The roaster sits behind the counter, so you can watch a 12-kilo batch turn from green to gold while you wait for your cortado. We buy shade-grown beans from estates in Chikmagalur, Coorg and Araku, and print the farm's name on every bag.

      Prefer a traditional decoction? Choose the South Indian filter grind when you order and we'll tuck in a brewing card.
